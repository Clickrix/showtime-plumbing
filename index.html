Common Questions

Quick answers to the plumbing questions we hear most. Still unsure? Call 562-822-5734 and ask us directly.

Do you offer emergency plumbing service?

Yes. Our emergency plumbing service runs 24 hours a day, 7 days a week, including weekends and holidays. Burst pipes, major leaks, and sewer backups get a fast response any time.

Can you repair my water heater the same day?

In most cases, yes. We stock common water heater parts and replacement units, so most repairs and standard replacements finish in a single visit.

What is hydro-jetting and when do I need it?

Hydro-jetting uses high-pressure water to scour the inside of sewer lines, clearing grease, roots, and scale. It is the best fix for recurring clogs and slow drains that a snake cannot clear.

Do I need to dig up my yard to fix a sewer line?

Usually not. Pipe bursting and epoxy spray lining replace or rebuild damaged sewer pipes with little or no digging, which protects your landscaping and driveway.

Are you licensed and insured?

Yes. Showtime Plumbing LLC is fully licensed (CA license 1070799) and insured, and every job is done to code with certified specialists.

How much does drain cleaning cost?

It depends on the clog and the line. We give an honest upfront price after diagnosing the problem, and we never start work without your approval.

Which areas do you serve?

We are based in La Puente and regularly serve El Monte, Rowland Heights, West Covina, Industry, and Baldwin Park, plus nearby San Gabriel Valley communities.
